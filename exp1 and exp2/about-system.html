About the System

The Automated Urine Test Strip Reader is a compact, point of care diagnostic technology developed to bring clinicalgrade, objective urinalysis directly to bedside and resource limited healthcare settings. By pairing a controlled illumination hardware unit with computer vision and machine learning models, the system transforms conventional dipstick testing into a fully automated, precise digital diagnostic tool.

Objectives

  • Eliminate Illumination and Human Error: Eliminate ambient lighting variability and subjective visual interpretation by capturing test strip images in a calibrated, light controlled enclosure.
  • Deliver Precise Quantitative Data: Map reagent pad color shifts to exact, continuous numerical analyte concentrations rather than broad categorical ranges.
  • Automate the Full Analytical Workflow: Streamline the diagnostic process into a single automated sequence: strip insertion, timed biochemical reaction, image capture, feature extraction, concentration computation, and digital report generation.
  • Process All 10 Reagent Pads Simultaneously: Automatically detect, isolate, and quantify all 10 reagent parameters (including glucose, protein, pH, ketones, and blood) concurrently.
  • Integrate with Healthcare Databases: Automatically sync encrypted test outputs, patient IDs, and timestamps with hospital information systems for long term time series health monitoring.

Working Principle

  • Controlled Environment Capture: The user inserts the urine dipped test strip into a custom, opaque "black box" enclosure equipped with brightness regulated LED strips and a Raspberry Pi camera module, ensuring uniform lighting free from external interference.
  • Automated Timed Sequence: Upon strip detection, an internal timer initiates a strict 120 second reaction wait time to ensure optimal biochemical color development across all pads before triggering image capture.
  • Computer Vision Segmentation: An integrated OpenCV pipeline identifies the horizontally aligned test strip, applies fixed geometry bounding boxes to isolate all 10 reagent pads, and extracts average RGB values into a multi dimensional feature vector.
  • Non Linear AI Regression: Advanced machine learning regression models (multidimensional polynomial interpolation / deep learning) process the complex, interdependent RGB color data to calculate exact numerical concentrations for each analyte.
  • Standalone On Device Execution: The entire end to end pipeline executes locally on an edge computing unit (Raspberry Pi 5)—requiring no external smartphones, laptops, or cloud processing to generate a complete digital report.

Benefits

  • Objective Accuracy: Replaces subjective color chart comparison with reliable, objective numerical results to reduce inter observer variability.
  • Point of Care Readiness: Designed as a fully self contained unit, making automated urinalysis accessible in rural, low resource, or mobile healthcare environments without laboratory infrastructure.
  • Complex Multi Parameter Intelligence: Accounts for non linear color responses and pad to pad interactions (crosstalk) across all 10 reagent fields simultaneously.
  • Seamless EHR Integration: Supports instant, encrypted data transfer to hospital databases, enabling seamless tracking of patient health trends over time.
  • Enhanced Clinical Decision Support: Highlights abnormal values against standard clinical reference intervals to give clinicians clear, actionable data.
