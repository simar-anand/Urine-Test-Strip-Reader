Urine Testing Procedure

  1. Collect a fresh urine sample in a clean container.
  2. Remove the urine test strip from its container.
  3. Dip the test strip completely into the urine sample.
  4. Remove the strip and allow excess urine to drain.
  5. Wait for the specified reaction time.
  6. Place the strip in the AI-enabled urine strip reader.
  7. Allow the system to analyse the test parameters.
  8. Review and record the test results.
